1. The screener QUESTION
2. The CANDIDATE ANSWER (drafted by another model)
3. RESUME FACTS (source of truth)

Classify whether the answer is fully supported. Never allow fabricated employers,
metrics, skills, degrees, titles, or dates.

Return ONLY JSON:
{

  "cited_facts": ["exact quotes from resume facts that support the answer"],
  "issues": ["human-readable problems if any"]
}

Rules:

- Any FABRICATED claim → passed=false.
